## Overview

**The Curriculum of the River** is a 300-day middle school mathematics sequence designed as an architectural, classical trivium. Built upon the standard of **17" × 11" landscape master broadsheets**, every daily lesson is completely self-contained upon a single folio plate, integrating panoramic visual models, formal mathematical vocabulary, operational invariance ladders, and authentic Pacific Northwest craft metrology.

### The Four-Stage Grounded Abstraction
Every lesson adheres to a four-stage epistemological progression:
1. **The Sensory Referent:** A physical, concrete phenomenon (e.g., timber joist spans, reservoir inflow, hydraulic line pressure, balance beams).
2. **The Disciplinary Name:** Precise classical mathematical terminology tethered directly to the referent (e.g., *Additive Inverse*, *Affine Translation*, *Extreme-Mean Proportion*).
3. **The Affixing Bridge:** Reversible operational mechanisms and algebraic checks proving structural symmetry.
4. **The Abstract Invariant:** The universal mathematical law ($y = mx + b$, $\frac{a}{b} \div \frac{c}{d} = \frac{ad}{bc}$, $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$), independent of context and true across all spaces.
