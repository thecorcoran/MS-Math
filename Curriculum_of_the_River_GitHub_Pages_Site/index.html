Epistemological Method

The Four-Stage Grounded Abstraction

How authentic mathematical understanding unfolds upon every broadsheet plate:

1

The Sensory Referent

Intuitive & Physical

Directly observed physical phenomena—reservoir inflow, balance beams, gear trains, laser transit benchmarks, or timber blocking.

2

The Disciplinary Name

Formal Precision

Bestowing exact classical mathematical vocabulary: Additive Inverse, Rate Quotient, Vertical Intercept, or Extreme-Mean Proportion.

3

The Affixing Bridge

Algebraic Tethering

Reversible dual-pan equilibrium checks and coordinate lattices proving that operations can be inverted and undone without loss of truth.

4

The Abstract Invariant

Universal Theorem

The generalized law ($y = mx + b$, $a/b \div c/d = ad/bc$, $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$) liberated from any single context, universally true in all spaces.
